VELZA GLOBAL ANNOUNCES TONINO LAMBORGHINI ROSSO CAFFÈ LAUNCH IN INDIA AND INDONESIA

Velza Global is excited to launch Tonino Lamborghini Rosso Caffè in India and Indonesia. The rollout is planned for Q2 2027. Bringing together Italian coffee culture, distinctive design, and a shared passion for quality, Velza Global and Tonino Lamborghini are introducing a new café experience to India and Indonesia.

Some things cannot be expressed but are only espresso-ed through a crafted experience for people who love to enjoy their coffee rituals, where they discover a space, a rare geography for their own thoughts and stillness, where they want to stay a little longer with themselves. Coffee has been a part of global culture and history, representing hospitality, custom, respect, and productivity. All over the world, saying “let’s get a coffee” is rarely about the beverage itself; it is more an open invitation for conversations, relationship-building, and community.

Now, Velza Global Group and Tonino Lamborghini S.p.A. are excited to announce Tonino Lamborghini Rosso Caffè.

“Our ambition is to offer Indian consumers an authentic expression of the Italian coffee lifestyle, combining quality, distinctive design, and a truly immersive hospitality experience,”

— Mr Tonino Lamborghini, Founder and President of Tonino Lamborghini S.p.A.

This will redefine the meaning of coffee culture in India and Indonesia; it’s a shared vision of both companies to bring premium Italian coffee and lifestyle culture to these high-growth Asian markets. The initiative represents a strategic alliance with Tonino Lamborghini beyond caffeinated beverages; it’s more. Discovering the flavours, details, and atmosphere of Italian coffee.

A Little Taste of Italy. A Lot to Savour

Italians have their own character and version of coffee, which celebrates simplicity, curation, and the uncompromised pleasure of taking time for yourself, which is desirable and feels unapologetic.

We are embodying these exact beliefs through Tonino Lamborghini Rosso Caffè, which simply combines the idea of traditional Italian coffee with discreet design, luxury hospitality, and seldom-seen taste.

This vision is not limited to the café model; it will leave conventional coffee shops behind and bring a larger lifestyle experience to India and Indonesia, with exceptional Italian architecture, high-contrast designs, and intentional detailing. Coffee is personal to everyone; they have their own taste and idea of enjoying it. After all, grabbing a cup of coffee? It’s not everyone’s cup of coffee.

India: Where Rooted in Traditional History Meets New-Gen Experience

Coffee culture in India is hitting an entirely new stride. It’s no longer just about the classic morning filter coffee. From historic coffee capitals to modern urban hubs, a new generation is rewriting the rules of the brew and turning the humble coffee shop into a design-led lifestyle destination.

Nowadays, consumers don’t just simply buy a product - they invest in the experience. The big shot here is getting a bold, unique flavour to a market that is always flourishing in its hunger for something different. In the varied landscape where people look for meaning in a product alongside value and experience, the real magic happens when you bring an unmatched, singular experience to an already rich and diverse coffee culture.

“India represents a dynamic and forward-looking market, where heritage, innovation and contemporary lifestyles come together. Following our successful collaboration with Velza Global in the caffeinated beverage sector, the introduction of Tonino Lamborghini Rosso Caffè marks a new step in our journey together,”

— Mr Tonino Lamborghini, Founder and President of Tonino Lamborghini S.p.A.

For Velza, this is a massive capitalization shift: As we are aware, modern consumers are hungry for unprecedented international concepts that come with astonishing designs, opulent quality, and sensational hospitality. The rollout is set to begin with an initial blueprint targeting 10 flagship Rosso Caffè locations across India’s major metropolitan hubs.

Indonesia’s Rise as a Coffee Powerhouse

Indonesia comes with another boom: a country with its own deeply rooted coffee custom. The idea is not just to introduce a new foreign concept in the market, but to bring Italian coffee philosophy into society as a daily essential ritual, a social matter, and to establish local identity.

“Indonesia’s vibrant and deeply rooted coffee culture makes the country a natural destination for Tonino Lamborghini Rosso Caffè and an exciting new chapter in our partnership with Velza Global. More than a traditional café, the project has been developed as a contemporary lifestyle destination, bringing together authentic Italian coffee culture, distinctive design, premium hospitality, and state-of-the-art professional equipment. Through a phased development of ten locations over the next three years, we aim to build a strong and recognizable presence in the Indonesian market, creating spaces where the bold character of our brand can engage with the energy and sophistication of local consumers,”

— Mr Tonino Lamborghini, Founder and President of Tonino Lamborghini S.p.A.

The growth plan outlines 10 flagship destinations across Indonesia within the next 3 years.

Both India and Indonesia are planning to establish 20 premier locations in total with a distinct character of their own.

Creating Destinations, Not Just Adding Locations

For Velza Global Group, this isn’t just about extending branches into a new market category. It is a continuation of our promise to bring magnificent, world-class lifestyle and consumer experiences straight to India and Indonesia.

“We are thrilled to deepen our partnership with Tonino Lamborghini as we bring TL Rosso Caffè to India. Our vision is to deliver an authentic Italian luxury ecosystem, combining world-class heritage with a dynamic lifestyle concept for India's modern consumers,”

— Ajit Narapareddy, CEO and CMD of Velza Global Group
