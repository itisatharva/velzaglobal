VELZA GLOBAL AND TONINO LAMBORGHINI PARTNER IN THE PHILIPPINES TO REDEFINE THE ITALIAN COFFEE EXPERIENCE WITH THE LAUNCH OF TL ROSSO CAFFÈ

Metro Manila, Philippines – August 4, 2026 – Velza Global and Tonino Lamborghini S.p.A. are proud to announce their exclusive partnership for the opening of the Tonino Lamborghini Rosso Caffè in the Philippines, marking the brand’s debut in the country’s premium coffee lifestyle segment.

Following the partnership for Tonino Lamborghini Energy Drinks in the Philippines and in India, the new agreement further strengthens the alliance between Velza Global and Tonino Lamborghini, expanding from luxury beverages to a complete Italian coffee lifestyle experience in the Philippines.

Tonino Lamborghini Rosso Caffè embodies the essence of Italian lifestyle through design, quality, innovation and an uncompromising attitude. The launch aims to introduce a distinctive coffee experience to the Philippine market, where authentic Italian coffee culture and contemporary lifestyle come together in an immersive destination.

“With TL Rosso Caffè, we bring to the Philippines a new expression of Italian lifestyle: bold, refined and deeply connected to the culture of authentic espresso. This project is not simply about coffee; it is about creating a destination where Italian taste, design and attitude come together in a distinctive experience. Southeast Asia has always shared a special connection with my brand, and anticipating new markets has always been part of my entrepreneurial vision. I’m pleased to continue our journey with Velza Global and to introduce the TL Rosso Caffè concept to the Philippines as a first mover in the country continuing our international growth journey without limits,” declared Mr. Tonino Lamborghini, Founder and President of Tonino Lamborghini S.p.A.

“We are pleased to expand our journey with Tonino Lamborghini in the Philippines once more. We want to make the entire eco-culture of Tonino Lamborghini available in the Philippines so that luxury and Italian pride come together in a complete ecosystem,” said Mr. Ajit Narapareddy, CEO of Velza Global Group.

Sophistication in every pour

Tonino Lamborghini Rosso Caffè was created to respond to the expectations of a refined international audience seeking more than a traditional coffee experience. The concept reflects the core values of the Tonino Lamborghini brand: unmistakable style, bold character and a strong commitment to Italian attitude.

Tonino Lamborghini Rosso Caffè is not just a café; it is a lifestyle destination. Defined by a minimalist, high-contrast luxury aesthetic, each venue is conceived as a carefully curated environment where design, hospitality and premium coffee culture meet. The spaces feature state-of-the-art professional equipment developed in collaboration with leading Italian partners.

With more than three decades of experience in the coffee industry, Tonino Lamborghini continues to evolve its vision of Italian coffee culture through the TL Rosso Caffè project, offering a distinctive format that brings together heritage, design and contemporary lifestyle.

TL Rosso Caffè project timeline

Velza Global plans to launch the first Tonino Lamborghini Rosso Caffè in the Philippines by the end of December 2026, with a strategic expansion plan to open a total of 10 locations over the next three years.
