TONINO LAMBORGHINI & VELZA GROUP – NEW PARTNERSHIP.

Velza Global, a leading diversified FMCG and Lifestyle company, and Tonino Lamborghini, the iconic Italian Lifestyle brand, today officially announced a strategic partnership to launch the Tonino Lamborghini Energy Drink exclusively in the Philippine market.

“We are pleased to welcome our new partner in the Philippines. Together, we will bring the energy, determination and the uncompromised spirit of my brand to a dynamic and fast-growing market. This marks the beginning of a shared journey driven by ambition and excellence.” said Mr. Tonino Lamborghini.

“We are proud to bring the energy and passion of the Lamborghini brand to the Philippines,” said Mr. Ajit Narapareddy, CEO, Velza Global Co. “This partnership between Tonino Lamborghini and Velza Global fuses Italian performance with Filipino ambition. We are not just launching a luxury beverage; we are delivering the fuel for a high-octane lifestyle to the Philippine market.”

Italian lifestyle Meets Filipino Ambition

Tonino Lamborghini Energy Drink is meticulously designed in Italy with an exclusive Filippino recipe for those who refuse to compromise. The product embodies the brand's core values: uncompromised style, dynamism, Italian attitude, and boldness, making it a distinctive choice in the luxury beverage market. Available in Original and Zero Sugar variants, the sleek, elegant can is adorned with the iconic Raging Bull emblem, reflecting the brand's unwavering commitment to Made in Italy excellence.

Availability

Tonino Lamborghini Energy Drink will be available from February 2026 exclusively through Velza Global's high-end network, including luxury hotel chains, exclusive members' clubs, and select luxury retail outlets across Metro Manila and other key cities.

About Velza Global Co.

Velza Global Co. is a diversified FMCG and lifestyle company with a vision to be Southeast Asia’s most agile and admired consumer company. From pantry to wardrobe, Velza delivers essential and aspirational products that shape modern Filipino lifestyles. With core segments in Beverages, Ready-to-eat foods, and Luxury apparel distribution, Velza is building a hybrid business model that blends fast-moving essentials with trend-setting luxury.

About Tonino Lamborghini S.p.A.

Since 1981, Tonino Lamborghini has been celebrated for innovative design and exclusive luxury. The brand has expanded from eyewear to watches, fashion accessories, and into sectors such as hospitality, real estate, total living, luxury beverages, and even electric golf carts - all reflecting the elegance and sophistication of Italian design. More information can be found at: www.lamborghini.it.
