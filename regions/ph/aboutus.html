Who We Are

Building a Premium Ecosystem

Velza Global is a premium consumer brand company specializing in the importation, manufacturing, distribution, and strategic development of internationally recognized luxury brands, headquartered in HongKong. The company specializes in the importation, manufacturing, distribution, and brand development of internationally recognized.
From luxury beverages and Italian spirits to coffee, hospitality, and lifestyle essentials, every portfolio we manage reflects our commitment to quality, authenticity, and long-term brand value.

Velza Global operates at the intersection of luxury branding, strategic global partnerships, and scalable distribution networks, bringing world-class products into the South East Asian market.

Our Story

Velza Global was founded on a simple belief:
Emerging markets deserve access to the same world-class brands, experiences, and standards enjoyed by the world's most established luxury economies.
Rather than simply importing products, we build complete ecosystems around every brand we represent; from manufacturing and distribution to retail experiences, marketing, and long-term brand growth.
Because true luxury is never just about products.
It's about creating experiences people remember.
